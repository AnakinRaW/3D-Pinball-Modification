# Playfield drawing

- **`playfield.svg` and `features.svg` are written by `tools/playfield/compose.py`** from `parts/` and `playfield.json`. A change goes into a part file or into the JSON, and the composed files are written again. `--check` has to pass afterwards.
- **A feature bubble stands for a row of `docs/features.md`** that has a place on the machine. Its number is the item's number in the root README's list, and its `targets` name placements or LEDs. The list in the README and `features` in the JSON change together. A feature that a part above it hides is shown through it: `feature_style` and `feature_defs` hold the see-through windows and the state layers, such as stored balls, that only the feature picture shows.
- **The kit manual's track map is the geometry's base.** It is an undistorted top view. Its pixels map to millimeters by scale alone, with the inner walls 230 mm apart. A rectified photo squeezes the top of the playfield and shifts raised ramps by parallax, so a photo places nothing.
- **A part file is one physical part.** Its outline comes first, and whatever is painted or cut on it lies inside that outline and is clipped to it. A part that has to move or hide as one is never split across files.
- **Parts that look the same share one file.** A new copy is a placement with `at`, `rotate`, `scale` and `mirror`, never a second file. A copy that differs in paint or text only takes a variant class or a `text` entry, as the score numbers do. A part is decided by what it is on the machine: the score star is an insert, and its number is paint on the playfield. A hole is cut into the playfield board and is drawn in the board's file. A shared part stands upright in its file, and each placement carries its angle.
- **An element a part names carries `data-name`**, and the drawing gives it the placement's id in front. A lamp or a GUI addresses it by that id.
- **A strip keeps its pitch of 6.25 mm.** A route that the strip covers only in part is shortened or lengthened in the JSON, and the LEDs are never spread to fit.
