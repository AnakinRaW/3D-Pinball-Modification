# QUARKZMAN FFC/FPC board and flat flex cable

The display's flat flex cable runs between two QUARKZMAN FFC/FPC connector boards. A3 sits on the display board, A4 beside the module.

## Connector board

Figures are quoted from the Amazon listing B0D846Z73P, which gives no drawing and no datasheet.

| Item | Listing |
|---|---|
| Socket | 20 contacts, 0.5 mm pitch, flip lock, one socket on the front |
| Back | pads for a 1.0 mm socket, left empty |
| Header | 2 × 10 pins, 2.54 mm pitch |
| Size | 26 × 33 mm |
| Pack | 4 boards |

The silkscreen numbers the header and the socket. The even pins sit on the row at the board's edge, and each odd pin sits beside its even pin, toward the socket. Header pin n carries socket contact n, and pin 1 lies at the end where the socket has contact 1. The header's position on the board is not stated and has not been measured.

## Flat flex cable

Figures are quoted from the Amazon listing B0G15RKF2G.

| Item | Listing |

The thickness at the contacts is not stated. Sockets of this kind take 0.3 mm.
