# BTF-Lighting WS2812B-2020 strip, 5 mm

BTF-Lighting makes this strip with WS2812B LEDs in the 2020 package, 160 to the meter, on a strip 5 mm wide. Each LED takes its color from the data line in green, red, blue order and passes the rest on.

## Current

One meter of it, 160 LEDs, was measured on a bench supply, with the meter in series in the strip's 5 V line. FastLED lit the first LED in one color at a time, at full brightness, and left the others dark.

| The first LED shows | Meter | One LED, the dark meter taken off |
|---|---|---|
| Black | 66.1 mA | |
| Red | 71.6 mA | 5.5 mA |
| Green | 71.7 mA | 5.6 mA |
| Blue | 71.6 mA | 5.5 mA |
| White | 82.5 mA | 16.4 mA |

The controller of an LED draws 0.41 mA, also while the LED is dark: the dark meter's 66.1 mA over its 160 LEDs.

The three colors add up to 16.6 mA, a little more than the 16.4 mA white draws.

## Data line

The strip runs straight from a Teensy 4.1 pin at 3.3 V, without a level shifter.

## Chip

The chip is unknown. Worldsemi's WS2812B-2020-V6 is ruled out: its datasheet tests it at 12 mA per color and gives below 1 µA dark.

## Fit

A cut LED is 5 mm wide and 6.25 mm long, 8.01 mm across its diagonal.
