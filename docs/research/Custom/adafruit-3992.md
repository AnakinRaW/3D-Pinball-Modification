# Adafruit 3992

The Adafruit 3992 is a push-pull solenoid, sold as Medium Push-Pull Solenoid 5V or 6V. Its armature pulls in at one end and pushes out at the other. A return spring brings the armature back when the coil is released.

Figures are quoted from the [Adafruit product page](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3992).

## Measurements

Voltages and currents are read off the lab supply's display. The coil's resistance is measured with a multimeter from the red to the black wire. That reading includes the meter's own cables.

| Measurement | Result |
|---|---|
| Coil resistance | 6.2 Ω |
| Current at 4.8 V | 0.8 A |
| Horizontal, with the spring | pulls at 5 V |
| Pulling upward against gravity, with the spring | does not pull at 5 V, pulls at 6 V |
| Pulling upward against gravity, spring removed | pulls, the supply displaying 4.4 V with the armature pulled in |
